Hero Arena Privacy Policy

November 23, 2026

This policy explains how Hero Arena Studio handles information in the Android game Hero Arena.

Request access or deletion

Local play and saved progress

Your hero name, progress, inventory, and game settings are saved on your device. Solo gameplay does not require you to provide an email address, phone number, password, or social account.

Multiplayer

When you create or join an internet room, Unity Authentication signs you in as a guest and assigns a Unity player ID. Unity Relay uses that ID, your IP address, and connection, activity, and performance information to connect players, operate the service, and monitor its reliability. Relay can derive an approximate location from connection information. It does not require your device's precise location.

The game exchanges your chosen hero display name, equipped items, and gameplay state with the other player so the match can run. Use a nickname if you prefer to keep your real name private. Internet matches use encrypted Relay transport. If you select local Wi-Fi play, the game instead exchanges match information directly on your local network.

Unity processes information for these services. See Unity's Privacy Policy. This version does not show ads or process in-app purchases.

Retention

Your device keeps local game data until you clear the app's data or remove its local save. Unity documents a 30-day retention period for Relay personal data. Unity Authentication retains the guest player ID by default until it is deleted. If you contact us, we use the address and details you provide to respond and handle your request.

Access and deletion requests

To ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your Hero Arena guest player, email mstfygt0@gmail.com. Include “Hero Arena” and the type of request. If you know your Unity player ID, include it so we can locate the relevant data. You can contact us even if you have uninstalled the game. We may ask for additional information to verify the request. Please do not send a password or verification code.

To delete progress stored on your Android device, use the device's app settings to clear Hero Arena's storage. This resets local characters and progress. Clearing local storage does not itself make a deletion request to Unity.

Age and contact

Hero Arena is intended for players aged 13 and older. Direct privacy or support questions to mstfygt0@gmail.com. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.
